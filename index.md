# Thumbsmart Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Thumbsmart (formerly JustPause) is designed to keep your data on your device.

## What we collect
Nothing. Thumbsmart has no account, no analytics, and no ads. We do not collect,
store, sell, or share any personal information.

## What stays on your device
Your focus schedules, the apps you choose, any websites you add, and your
history of pauses are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage.
This information is never sent to us or anyone else. Reminders and other
notifications are created on your device; there is no server sending them.
You can export or erase your history at any time in Settings > Your data.

## Screen Time
If you use Block, Thumbsmart uses Apple's Screen Time API to block the apps
you choose, and their websites, during the times you set. Apple gives
Thumbsmart only anonymous tokens for your selections, not the names of your
apps or what you do in them. Thumbsmart never sees or records your browsing.
This stays on your device. You can turn off Thumbsmart's Screen Time access at
any time in Settings > Screen Time.

## Purchases
Thumbsmart Plus, including promo codes, is handled by Apple. We never see
your payment details. Apple's privacy policy covers that transaction.
